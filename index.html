Wei Tian is currently a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the database group of the Big Data Institute in Shenzhen University. He published several research papers in top-tier conferences and journals in the field of database and data mining, such as VLDB, ICDE and SIGKDD. He received the PhD degree in computing from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, under the supervison of Prof. Jieming Shi and Prof. Man Lung Yiu. He obtained a Master degree in Computer Technology supervised by Prof. Min Zhu, and a Bachelor degree in Computer Science both from Sichuan University. Besides, He is a CCF member and an ACM member.

Research Interests

My research focuses on data-driven algorithms and techniques that bring big data back to a human scale. I study how to make big data empower everyone for optimal decision making and improve every individual citizen’s daily life. I am particularly interested in the following research directions:
  • Spatial and Temporal Data Management
  • Urban Computing
  • Geo-spatial Data Visualization
  • Low-altitude GIS
